# Smalls

> Smalls is a New York City based direct-to-consumer pet food company founded in 2017 by Matt Michaelson and Calvin Bohn. It sells gently cooked human-grade fresh cat food, freeze-dried raw and treats on a personalized monthly subscription built from an onboarding quiz, shipped nationwide, and since 2023 also sold through brick-and-mortar retail and wholesale. Smalls does not publish a developer program, API, SDK or agent-facing commerce endpoint.

## APIs

Smalls publishes no public API. A contract-discovery pass on 2026-08-02 probed
smalls.com, www.smalls.com, api.smalls.com, developers.smalls.com and
shop.smalls.com for OpenAPI/Swagger, GraphQL introspection, an MCP server, an A2A
agent card, and the /.well-known/ discovery surface, and found none. The storefront
is a Remix single-page application on Netlify backed by Contentful; its router
answers unknown paths with a 200 HTML shell, so 200 responses on paths such as
/openapi.json or /llms.txt are catch-alls, not documents.
